# Consumer withdrawal information — draft

Dated 28 October 2026. Complete against the actual checkout and online withdrawal
function and obtain legal review before activation.

A consumer may withdraw from the purchase within 14 days of contract formation
without providing a reason. The period requires the legally prescribed information
to have been supplied; statutory extensions for missing information remain intact.

Send a clear statement of withdrawal by email to support@kalanis.de or by post to
Marvin Kalani, Zum Bruch 3a, 28816 Stuhr, Germany. Sending it within the period is
sufficient. The optional form below is a convenience, not a requirement.

Following a valid withdrawal, received payments are refunded without undue delay
and no later than 14 days after the withdrawal statement is received. The original
payment method is normally used; another method requires express agreement. No
refund fee is charged. Any further statutory rights remain unaffected.

This draft does not require early loss of withdrawal rights for immediate digital
delivery. Downloading, installing or using the program alone is not treated as a
waiver.

**Required before selling:** insert the actual accessible online withdrawal
function. It must accept an unambiguous withdrawal and promptly provide a durable
receipt of the statement. A `mailto:` link alone does not replace this function.
