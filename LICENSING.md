## What is a user?

A user is a natural person, not a machine, account, model, container or agent.
One developer with a laptop, home workstation, two containers and several
coding agents needs one seat when paid production use applies. A team of five
people submitting requests through one shared server needs five seats. Customers
who only receive the team's generated invoices or website do not need seats.
Purchases for teams can select the quantity at checkout. Assign seats locally;
do not send us your users' names. Keep the receipt as your license record.

The license covers all 1.x versions, starting with **1.2610.28**. There is no
activation or online license check. Pairing codes authenticate peers and are
independent of payment. The self-assessed threshold is a license condition;
the absence of monitoring does not remove that condition.

and Additional Use Grant specify this project's policy.

Basalt, ThinkRail, Pi, OpenCode and other dependencies retain their licenses; see

and documentation only.

## Payment and privacy

Checkout is a separate, user-initiated visit to Stripe. Stripe processes payment
and billing information; it does not receive prompts from k-lani-link. There is
no Stripe SDK, payment callback, tracking pixel or license check in the inference
containers. The application works independently of the purchase page.

Contact: support@kalanis.de.

Voluntary support is also welcome through [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/mkalani).
A support contribution is separate from purchasing a license and does not
replace a required commercial license.
